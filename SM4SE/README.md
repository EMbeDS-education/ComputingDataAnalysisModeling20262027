Course Simultation methods for statistics and econometrics

The course offers an investigation of simulation methods in econometrics, in particular the Monte Carlo method, for evaluating the performance of estimators and test procedures, as well as for solving models that do not have a closed form solution. Attention will be devolted to the design, analysis, reporting, and presentation of the results. 

Basic knowledge of statistical and econometric analysis is required.
